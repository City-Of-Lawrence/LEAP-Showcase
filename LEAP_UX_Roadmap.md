# LEAP Portal: UX & Workflow Improvement Roadmap

This document summarizes the strategic UX and workflow improvements for the Lawrence Energy Affordability Project (LEAP) portal. These recommendations focus on closing the "handoff cliff," reducing user attrition, and positioning the City as a continuous, trusted authority.

## 1. Bridging the "Handoff Cliff"
The transition from your portal to external state programs (e.g., Mass Save) is the highest-risk moment for user drop-off.

* **The Continuity Strategy (Copy Refinement):**
    * **Current State:** The "You’re All Set!" screen signals an end to the process.
    * **Improvement:** Reframe the copy to emphasize that this is a *continued* City-led process.
    * **Example Copy:** "Registration Complete: Now, Complete Your Official Enrollment." Include clear body copy explaining that the City is still monitoring their progress.
* **The Safety Net (In-Form Support):**
    * Include a "Stuck on the Mass Save site?" section on the redirection page. 
    * Provide direct contact information (Phone/Email) for your Energy Advocate to provide immediate comfort if the external form becomes overwhelming.
* **The Bridge Graphic:**
    * Visually represent the partnership on the redirect screen using the City of Lawrence logo and the Mass Save logo connected by an arrow. This reinforces that the resident remains within the City's "trust umbrella" even when navigating an external state portal.
* **The Tab Trick:**
    * Configure all "Complete Enrollment" buttons to **open in a new browser tab**. This ensures the LEAP portal remains open in the original tab, allowing users to easily return to your site for support or to retrieve their Advocate’s contact info.

## 2. Engagement & Retention
Improving how you capture and support users who are struggling or dropping off.

* **Exit Analytics & "Save Progress":**
    * Implement tracking to identify exactly where users drop off in your forms.
    * If a user pauses, trigger a "Need Help? Talk to an Advocate" prompt. 
    * If possible, introduce a "Save Progress" feature so users can return to their application later without restarting.
* **Automated "First Touch":**
    * Even if the Advocate is busy, the system should not be silent. Upon registration, send an automated SMS or email *immediately* confirming receipt and setting expectations for when a human will reach out. This prevents the "black hole" feeling.

## 3. Clarity & Content Strategy
Addressing the "Supply vs. Efficiency" confusion.

* **The "One-Sentence" Rule:**
    * Add tooltips or a small FAQ section below program cards to distinguish between efficiency (using less energy) and supply (paying less for energy). 
    * **Suggested Copy:** *"Efficiency helps you use less energy; Lawrence Energy Choice helps you pay less for the energy you use. Most families benefit from both."*

## 4. Operational Sustainability
Ensuring the human element remains scalable.

* **Advocate Capacity Management:**
    * As the volume of registrants increases, the Energy Advocate will face a bottleneck. 
    * Build a dashboard that alerts the Advocate to "at-risk" users (those who started but did not complete the handoff). This allows the Advocate to perform proactive outreach rather than reactive support.

***

*This roadmap should be implemented in phases, prioritizing the "Handoff Cliff" refinements first, as these have the highest impact on your conversion rates and resident trust.*
